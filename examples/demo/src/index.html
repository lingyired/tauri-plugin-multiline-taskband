Multiline Taskband

Taskbar overlay console · demo v0.2.0

About

This demo drives 5 instances (mb-1 … mb-5). Each renders two lines of text on the Windows taskbar; click an item on the taskbar — or its gear here — to open its settings popup. Everything you change is saved automatically and restored on the next launch.

Left instances hug the taskbar's left edge, right instances sit next to the notification area (tray). Rows are drag-reorderable — grab a row's grip, or press ↑/↓ on it; the taskbar follows the list order within each side. The spacing controls push each group away from its edge — e.g. to make room for TrafficMonitor.
